Spring Herb Chicken with Asparagus, Peas & Lemon Butter Sauce

A delightful spring dish with fresh herbs and vibrant vegetables, perfect for a light dinner or a special occasion.

Total Time: 50 minutes

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Servings: 4

Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Ingredients

For the Chicken:

  • 4 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs or breasts
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh chopped thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 1 tbsp fresh chopped parsley
  • Zest of 1 lemon

For the Vegetables:

  • 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen peas
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 2 spring onions or scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Preparation

  1. Pat chicken dry and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, thyme, parsley, and lemon zest.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken 4–5 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  3. In the same skillet, add more oil if needed. Sauté asparagus for 3 minutes. Add peas, radishes, and onions. Cook until just tender.
  4. Remove vegetables and add butter to skillet. Sauté garlic, then stir in lemon juice, broth, and mustard if using. Simmer to reduce.
  5. Return chicken to the skillet to coat in sauce. Serve over vegetables with fresh herbs on top.

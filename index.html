Spring Herb Chicken with Asparagus, Peas & Lemon Butter Sauce

Servings: 4
Cook Time: 40 minutes
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Course: Main Course
Cuisine: American

Recipe Overview

This dish is a delightful combination of juicy chicken, vibrant asparagus, and sweet peas, all brought together with a tangy lemon butter sauce. It's a perfect springtime meal that is both healthy and delicious.

Pair it with a light salad or some crusty bread for a complete meal. Enjoy the fresh flavors of spring with this easy-to-follow recipe!

Cooking Tips

  • For extra flavor, marinate the chicken in the herb mixture for a few hours before cooking.
  • Feel free to substitute the vegetables with your favorites or whatever is in season.
  • If you prefer a thicker sauce, let it simmer longer to reduce further.

Ingredients

For the chicken:

  • 4 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs or breasts
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh chopped thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
  • 1 tbsp fresh chopped parsley
  • Zest of 1 lemon

For the vegetables:

  • 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed and cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen peas
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 2 spring onions or scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

For the lemon butter sauce:

  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/4 cup chicken broth (or dry white wine)
  • Optional: 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • Fresh parsley or mint, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Pat chicken dry and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, thyme, parsley, and lemon zest.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken 4–5 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  3. In the same skillet, add more oil if needed. Sauté asparagus for 3 minutes. Add peas, radishes, and onions. Cook until just tender.
  4. Remove vegetables and add butter to skillet. Sauté garlic, then stir in lemon juice, broth, and mustard if using. Simmer to reduce.
  5. Return chicken to the skillet to coat in sauce. Serve over vegetables with fresh herbs on top.
